How to use this page

  1. 1

    Browse

    Scroll through the list, or search for a job title, a skill or something you enjoy doing.

  2. 2

    Read the details

    Open any career to see what the work involves, which skills help and the usual way in.

  3. 3

    Check the official source

    Entry rules for exams and courses such as NEET, CA and UPSC change from time to time, so confirm them on the official website before you plan.

About this page

CareerPath is a small project that helps students learn what different professions involve. It is a starting point, not personal career advice, so talk to teachers, family and people who do the work as well.
